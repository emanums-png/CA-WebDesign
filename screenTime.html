Screen-Time & Well Being

Why it matters

Phones, Laptops, and TVs are part of everyday life used for studying, working, and staying in touch with others. The screens themselves aren't the problem. Rather, spending too much time in front of them especially at night can affect your sleep, mood, concentration, and even your eyes. Small changes, such as taking breaks and keeping your phone away at bedtime, can make a big difference.

Did you know?

Looking at a screen can reduce how often you blink by a third to a half, which leaves your eyes dry and tired.
