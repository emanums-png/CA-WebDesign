Sleep & Productivity

Why Sleep Matters

Sleep is the main base for Good Health and Well Being. Adults need at least 7 hours of sleep every night, but 1 out of 3 adults don't get that. A bad night of sleep not only affects your health, but also your mood and efficiency during studies and work.

Learn More
How much should I sleep?

Adults (18-60 years) should sleep 7 hours or more per night. Teenagers should sleep around 8 to 10 hours.

Learn More
Health Risks

Bad sleep is related to obesity, type 2 diabetes, heart disease and depression.

Learn More
The Cost for Productivity

The lack of sleep costs around 411 billion dollars per year to the US economy, causing around 1.2 million work days lost.

Learn More

Tips for Better Sleep

  • Sleep and wake up at the same time every day, including weekends.
  • Keep the room quiet, dark and cool during your sleep.
  • Remove electronics from the room.
  • Avoid heavy meals close to bed time.
  • Avoid caffeine and alcohol before bed time.
  • Exercise during the day.
  • Get some natural light during the day.
  • Keep naps short during the day.
  • If the problems persist, see a doctor.
