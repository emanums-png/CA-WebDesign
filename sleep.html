Sleep & Productivity

Why Sleep Matters

Sleep is the main base for Good Health and Well Being. Adults need at least 7 hours of sleep every night, but 1 out of 3 adults don't get that. A bad night of sleep not only affects the health, but the humor and efficiency during studies and work.

Learn more (CDC)
How much should I sleep?

Adults (18-60 years) should sleep 7 hours or more per night. Teenagers should sleep around 8 to 10 hours.

Learn more (CDC)
Health Risks

Bad sleep is related to obesity, type 2 diabetes, heart diseases and depression.

Learn more (CDC)
The Cost for Productivity

The lack of sleep costs around 411 billion dollars per year for the USA economy, causing around 1,2 billion work days lost.

Learn more (RAND)

Tips for Better Sleep

  • Sleep and wake up in the same time every day, including weekends.
  • Keep the room silent, dark and fresh during your sleep.
  • Remove electronics from the room.
  • Avoid heavy meals close to bed time.
  • Avoid caffeine and alcohol before bed time.
  • Exercise yourself during the day.
  • Get some natural light during the day.
  • Take short naps during the day.
  • If the problems persist, find a doctor.
Learn more (CDC)
