Mental Health

Mental Health is Health Too!

The UN's SDG wants to promote Mental Health and Well Being by 2030. Almost 1 in 7 people in the world live with mental health problems, anxiety and depression, those are the most common ones. There are some effective treatments but the majority of people don't have access.

Learn More Learn More
A Global Challenge

Without treatment, mental disorders can disrupt daily life and cause suffering for a long time. A lot of people also suffer from stigma and discrimination.

Learn More
Job Impact

Depression and anxiety cause around 12 billion lost work days per year and cost around 1 trillion dollars to the global economy.

9 Steps for Mental Well-Being

  • Connect yourself with people like: Family, friends and colleagues.
  • Keep active with hobbies and activities you like.
  • Learn new things to gain more confidence in yourself.
  • Help others, little kindness gestures always count.
  • Pay attention to the present, don't overthink about the future.
  • Get a good amount of sleep.
  • Speak freely about how you feel.
  • Ask for help as soon as possible, treatment works.
  • If you are going through difficulties in your life, talk with someone you can trust.
