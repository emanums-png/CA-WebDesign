What is SDG 3?

The Sustainable Development Goal 3 (SDG 3) aims to ensure healthy lives and improve the well-being of everyone, of all ages. It is one of the 17 goals adopted by the United Nations in 2015, as part of the 2030 Agenda. One of the targets is to promote mental health and well-being. This website focuses on day-to-day habits that help people of any age feel better, physically and mentally.
